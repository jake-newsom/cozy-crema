Privacy Policy

Cozy Crema Cafe · Effective October 23, 2026

The short version

Cozy Crema Cafe has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your café is saved on your own phone. The only data that leaves your device is what's needed to process and restore the optional Full Café purchase.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (drinks served, tips, recipes, décor and settings) is stored only on your device. We never receive it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

Full Café is bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google handle payment, and we never see your payment details or your name. Their privacy policies cover that part: Apple, Google.

We use RevenueCat to check whether you own Full Café and to restore it. To do that, RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous ID it creates for your install, your purchase history for this app, and basic technical details sent with each request (such as app version, operating system, store country and IP address). This data is not linked to your name or email. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We don't sell or share your data.

Children

Cozy Crema Cafe is made for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can delete your game data at any time by deleting the app. To ask about, or delete, purchase records held by RevenueCat, email us at cozycremacafe@gmail.com.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Questions? Email cozycremacafe@gmail.com.
